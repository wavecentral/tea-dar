About this data

Compiled from publicly available SBEC Disciplinary Action Report PDFs published by the Texas Education Agency at tea.texas.gov. Each row represents one sanction action; an educator may appear more than once (e.g., a suspension followed by a later reinstatement). Allegation codes and school-district information are only published in FY 2025-2026 reports onwards.

The brief explanations of allegation codes shown in this app are best-effort inferences from the code abbreviations; refer to the official TEA documentation for definitive descriptions.
